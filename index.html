Installing on an iPod touch or iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Download.
  2. Tap the Share button in Safari’s bottom bar, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Feather Fling from your Home Screen once while on Wi-Fi. After that it plays offline.

To remove it, hold the icon until it wiggles and tap the X.
